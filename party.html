Your Prep

A few simple steps so your look wears beautifully all day.

Skin Prep, Day Of
  • Arrive with clean, freshly washed skin
  • Apply a light moisturizer only
  • Avoid heavy creams, facial oils, or SPF the morning of your application

Your skin will be professionally prepped during your appointment.

Skincare & Treatments
  • Avoid starting any new skincare routine within 4 to 6 weeks of the wedding
  • Retinol or prescription skincare, chemical peels, microneedling, laser treatments, injectables, or dermatology services? Add them to your notes above so your artist knows.
Hair Prep
  • If we are styling your hair: come with clean, dry hair and no product
  • If we are doing your makeup only: hair should be dry and mostly styled, unless we have talked through something different
The Morning Of
  • Be on time and ready for your scheduled application
  • Delays or unpreparedness may shorten application time

A calm, unrushed experience is intentional.

Makeup artistry is collaborative and tailored to you. Results may vary based on skin type, lighting, weather, and environment.
