Room Four

Skin & Hair Prep

How to arrive ready for your best application
Guide

A few simple steps make a beautiful difference in how your look wears and photographs. Your guests have their own version of this on their pages.

Skin Prep, Day Of
  • Arrive with clean, freshly washed skin
  • Apply a light moisturizer only
  • Avoid heavy creams, facial oils, or SPF the morning of your application

Your skin will be professionally prepped during your appointment.

Skincare & Treatments
  • Avoid starting any new skincare routine within 4 to 6 weeks of your wedding
  • Let us know about any retinol or prescription skincare
  • Let us know about any chemical peels, microneedling, or laser treatments
  • Let us know about any injectables or dermatology services

If dermaplaning is planned (highly recommended), complete it before your preview, not after.

Your Preview
  • Wear white or light-colored clothing
  • Prepare your skin as it will be on the wedding day
  • Complete spray tans and brow shaping before your preview

Inspiration photos are welcome. Every look is customized to your features and environment.

Hair Prep
  • If we are styling your hair: come with clean, dry hair and no product
  • If we are doing your makeup only: hair should be dry and mostly styled, unless we have talked through something different
Wedding Morning
  • Be on time and ready for your scheduled application
  • Delays or unpreparedness may shorten application time

A calm, unrushed experience is intentional.

Your Getting-Ready Space
  • A clean, indoor space
  • A table or counter surface
  • Access to natural light, a window is best
  • A chair for makeup clients. Please let us know if this cannot be accommodated.

Makeup artistry is collaborative and tailored to you. Results may vary based on skin type, lighting, weather, and environment.
